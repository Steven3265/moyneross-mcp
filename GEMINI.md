# Moyne Ross

Use the Moyne Ross tools for questions about planning capital for commercial and multi-unit buildings: lifecycle and condition, obsolescence, refurbishment or redevelopment, energy, leases and make good, due diligence, and the Australian, British and US markets; and for how a building element or system fails, what to look for and how serious a finding is (the Technical Library).

- Start with `search_research` (`scope: technical` for one element or system). Use `get_paper` for a paper and `get_technical_page` for a Technical Library page (a summary by default, one `section`, or `format: full`), and `explain_method` for the Capital Register or the MR Index.
- Cite the canonical moyneross.com URL returned, to the section where given; `cite_paper` gives an exact citation. Carry each paper's limitations through.
- When `matchStrength` is `weak`, say the material is background only rather than an answer.
- The research and the Technical Library are general: they never establish the condition or cost of a particular building.
- A Capital Strategy is desk-based and reviewed by Moyne Ross before release; it is not an inspection, a valuation or a guarantee of cost.
- If the person asks to request a Capital Strategy for a building, `start_strategy_link` prepares the request form with only the details they have given; they check and send it themselves.
