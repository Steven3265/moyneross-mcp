For forty years buildings were priced as income wrappers, bonds with a roof. That arrangement is loosening. Capital, policy and energy are moving back to the physical, and value follows what a building can do and how well its owner understands it. **Every building is a stock of decisions, and reading it as capital is the discipline that matters now.**

[Moyne Ross](https://moyneross.com/about) reads buildings that way. It prepares ten-year [Capital Strategies](https://moyneross.com/insights/what-a-capital-strategy-is) for owners, investors, asset managers and their advisers, and publishes the thinking and the technical ground behind them, openly, so that people and the assistants they use can draw on it.

This repository is the **Moyne Ross connector**: the whole public body of work, served to AI assistants over the Model Context Protocol. Ask a question in Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP client and the answer comes from Moyne Ross, cited to the exact section on moyneross.com.

Every finding is graded on one scale, so a page's judgement maps straight onto a line of a Capital Register:

| Situation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Safety** | Can hurt someone now. Make safe immediately. |
| **Failure** | Failed or failing, and damaging the building. Repair now. |
| **Condition** | Distress present and progressing. Repair in the near term. |
| **Lifecycle** | No current distress; renewal falls due on age and exposure. |
| **Deadline** | A regulator, ordinance or order sets the date. |
| **Check** | The sign warrants investigation before works are sized. |
| **Nil** | Cosmetic or stable. |

## How Moyne Ross works

A **Capital Strategy** is a ten-year view of what one building is likely to need, when, and at what cost. It is built on two things published in full:

- **[The Capital Register](https://moyneross.com/methodology/capital-register)**: the line-by-line document behind every strategy, with condition, action, timing, cost, impact and confidence on every row and the cost basis behind every figure.
- **[The MR Index](https://moyneross.com/methodology/mr-index)**: how much of the building needs attention, how serious it is and how soon, read element by element onto one band from MR 1 to MR 5. No costs take part.

See one: the [sample Capital Strategy](https://moyneross.com/samples/capital-strategy), a real strategy for an industrial warehouse and office in outer-eastern Melbourne with identifying details changed. $331,225 of planning allowances over ten years, MR 2.

The relationship has [three stages](https://moyneross.com/#how-we-work), each available on its own: **your strategy**, prepared and reviewed by Moyne Ross before release; **working it through**, where the questions it raises are settled with the owner's plans and records; and **ongoing**, where the strategy is kept as a [living record](https://moyneross.com/insights/the-living-record).

**[Start your strategy →](https://moyneross.com/contact)** An address is enough. Assistants connected here can open the form already filled in with the details a person has given; nothing is sent until the person sends it.

For individual homes in Australia, see [Homechecker](https://homechecker.com.au), by Moyne Ross.

## For developers

**Version:** `1.1.0` · **Protocol:** MCP `2026-07-28`, with stateless 2025-era compatibility · **Endpoint:** `https://mcp.moyneross.com/mcp` · **Registry:** `io.github.Steven3265/moyneross`

Public, read-only, and separate from every client system. The server bundles a snapshot of public files from moyneross.com; it has no route to the Moyne Ross portal, client strategies, payments or any database.

### Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `search_research` | Ranked search of the papers, the Technical Library, the methodology and the sample (`scope: technical` for the library alone). Every answer carries canonical URLs, a **match strength** with the evidence for it (distinctiveness-weighted coverage, matched and missing words), and, when the match is weak, questions the corpus does answer. Practitioner shorthand is understood (capex, dilaps, MEES, LL97, TDD). `response_format: detailed` adds a citation per result. |
| `list_papers` | The papers in reading order, a page at a time (`cursor`), optionally for one part (I to VII). |
| `get_paper` | One paper by slug, CMP number or URL. A summary by default (question, answer, summary, limitations, anchored sections); `section` for one section; `format: full` for the whole text. Always with a citation. |
| `explain_method` | The Capital Register or the MR Index: the definition and sections by default, `section` or `format: full` for more. |
| `get_sample_strategy` | A real Capital Strategy with identifying details changed: the summary, or the register as an overview by section or one section's lines with their cost basis. |
| `list_technical_pages` | The Technical Library in framework order (Substructure to Environmental), a page at a time, by section or page type, with the severity scale every page grades findings on. |
| `get_technical_page` | One library page by slug, element name or URL. A summary by default (question, answer, where it applies, limitations, sources, anchored sections); `section` for one section (`notice`, `severity`, `remedies`, `misreads` and the page's own topic sections); `format: full` for the whole page, a part at a time on a long page. Always with a citation. |
| `trace_connections` | The links between papers and library: from a paper, the library pages it rests on (with the section and why) and the papers on the same ground; from a library page, the papers grounded in it, related pages, and its spaces or elements. Exact links, not a search. |
| `building_brief` | For a kind of building (`commercial`, `industrial`, `institutional`, `residential-multi`): its spaces and the elements in play, in framework order and a page at a time, each with its service-life link, threshold counts by market and grounding papers. Narrow by `spaces`, `framework_section` or `market`. A map, never an assessment. |
| `compare_technical_sections` | One section side by side across two to five library pages (`service-life`, `severity`, `what-to-ask-for`, `misreads` and the rest), or `thresholds` by market. |
| `translate_term` | What a building element, lease term, code or report is called in Australia, the United Kingdom and the United States, from the published glossary (lift and elevator, make good and dilapidations), with the library pages that cover it. |
| `cite_paper` | An exact citation, to a paper or library page or one section, in BibTeX, RIS, CSL-JSON, APA or Harvard, generated rather than retyped by the model. |
| `working_with_moyne_ross` | How engagements work and the published terms. Not offered on `/mcp/openai` or to ChatGPT. |
| `start_strategy_link` | For a person who asks to request a Capital Strategy for one building: the request form link with the details they gave. Nothing is sent. Not offered on `/mcp/openai` or to ChatGPT. |

Every tool is read-only, with all four annotations set explicitly, input and output schemas, and icons. **Prompts:** `answer_from_research`, `compare_papers` (with paper autocompletion), `brief_on_topic` and `read_a_building_element` (with library autocompletion). **Resources:** `moyneross://catalogue`, the templates `moyneross://papers/{slug}` and `moyneross://technical/{slug}` (with autocompletion), `moyneross://methods/{capital-register|mr-index}` and `moyneross://sample`. Results link to them with `resource_link` blocks.

**Interactive views (MCP Apps).** In hosts that support MCP Apps (Claude, ChatGPT, VS Code, Microsoft 365 Copilot and others), `building_brief`, `compare_technical_sections` and `trace_connections` render as a view inside the conversation: the brief by framework section with threshold counts, a filter and paging; sections side by side, tables included; and the connections, walkable by clicking. One self-contained page with no network access, themed by the host; other hosts show the same answer as text.

**Asking which market.** Thresholds apply only in their own market. When they are asked for without a market, a client that can show a form is asked which market the building is in (AU, UK or US); otherwise, or if the person declines, every market's values come back labelled.

#### Built for every assistant's limits

Default answers stay under 20,000 characters (GitHub Copilot keeps them inline), every answer's text under 40,000 (Gemini CLI's truncation point), and the tools that can return a full paper declare `anthropic/maxResultSizeChars` so Claude Code keeps them inline too. Summaries come first; sections, full text and the register's lines are one argument away.

#### Citations that survive

The papers have 400 anchored sections between them and the library 1,491, matching the anchors on moyneross.com, so a claim can be cited to the exact section. Citations carry the corpus version (for example `2026-11-08.70433cca`: date and content fingerprint), so the cited text can always be identified.

### How it stays right

- **One corpus, three readers.** The snapshot is built from files the website's own publishing scripts produce (`/insights/export.json` with each paper's links into the library, `/insights/search-index.json`, `/technical/export.json`, `/llms.txt`, the methodology and sample pages). The ranking of the papers, methodology and sample is a faithful port of the website's public retrieval benchmark, on the website's own statistics, so the benchmark, the site search and this server rank them alike; library pages are scored beside them (see [docs/BENCHMARK.md](docs/BENCHMARK.md)).
- **Deterministic snapshot.** `data/corpus.json` is dated from the content (the latest sitemap date), never the clock. A daily workflow rebuilds it and opens a pull request only when moyneross.com has actually changed.
- **Checked on every change** (`npm run check`): release metadata (including the Agent Skills and Agent Plugins rules); skill copies; snapshot validation (structure, no secrets, none of the sample's real identifiers); 33 core tests; the retrieval gate (one evaluation of 727 cases in `data/retrieval-cases.json` and the website's 56-question benchmark, also used by the calibrator: website questions, practitioners' shorthand, building elements scoped and unscoped, pinned rankings, weak, empty and 122 off-topic questions, two reported sets of wrong-page and authored-FAQ questions, and 310 field questions in askers' own words across the three markets, reported and never gating; see docs/BENCHMARK.md); the protocol suite (2026-07-28 cache fields, ordering, result metadata and error codes; annotations, icons and size ceilings on every tool; prompts and completion; neutral answers on every surface; research only on the OpenAI surface; the paper–library graph tools; the interactive view; asking which market); the REST suite; and per-client answer-size budgets, hostile input and speed. The official MCP conformance suite runs in its own workflow (25 live checks). Each release is a GitHub Release with a CycloneDX SBOM.

### Boundaries and privacy

The research and the Technical Library are general; the library describes how elements behave and carries no prices. A Capital Strategy is desk-based and reviewed by Moyne Ross before release; it is not an inspection, a valuation or a guarantee of cost. The server cannot assess a building, order anything, access client data or modify any system.

Telemetry is operational only: protocol method, tool name, query length, scope, counts, match strength, a coarse client family, outcome and duration. Questions, addresses, names, emails and IP addresses are not logged by the application. Privacy: [moyneross.com/privacy#connector](https://moyneross.com/privacy#connector).
