For forty years buildings were priced as income wrappers, bonds with a roof. That arrangement is loosening. Capital, policy and energy are moving back to the physical, and value follows what a building can do and how well its owner understands it. **Every building is a stock of decisions, and reading it as capital is the discipline that matters now.**

[Moyne Ross](https://moyneross.com/about) reads buildings that way. It prepares ten-year [Capital Strategies](https://moyneross.com/insights/what-a-capital-strategy-is) for owners, investors, asset managers and their advisers, and publishes the thinking and the technical ground behind them, openly, so that people and the assistants they use can draw on it.

This repository is the **Moyne Ross connector**: the whole public body of work, served to AI assistants over the Model Context Protocol. Ask a question in Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP client and the answer comes from Moyne Ross, cited to the exact section on moyneross.com.
