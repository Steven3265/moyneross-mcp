# Capital Strategy hand-over

A Capital Strategy is a ten-year view of what a building is likely to need, when and at what cost, line by line in a Capital Register with an MR Index. It is desk-based, built from the address, public records, imagery and any documents supplied, and reviewed by Moyne Ross before release.

1. Check fit: commercial or multi-unit property (office, industrial, retail, mixed use, multi-unit residential, or a portfolio) in Australia, the United Kingdom or the United States. For a house or apartment, point to Homechecker (https://homechecker.com.au) instead.
2. If useful, show what they would receive with `get_sample_strategy`, and the terms with `working_with_moyne_ross`.
3. State the terms accurately: the strategy is released once they agree the terms and pay a fixed fee of 950 in local currency (AUD, GBP or USD). Anything beyond it is scoped for the building and agreed before it begins. Do not quote other prices, discounts or turnaround times.
4. Call `start_strategy_link` with only the details the person has given you: the building's address at least, and their name, email, organisation, role, building type or a short note if they offered them. Never invent or guess a detail.
5. Give them the link and say what happens: it opens the Start your strategy form already filled in; they check it and send it themselves. Nothing is sent on their behalf.

Do not describe a Capital Strategy as an inspection, a valuation or a guarantee of cost. If the tools are unavailable, give the form address https://moyneross.com/contact and the guide for assistants at https://moyneross.com/assistants.
